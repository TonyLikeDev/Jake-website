## Part 4 — Terms & Policies (Footer Link Pages)

### Class Hosting & Onboarding
Hosted on **ClassIn**. Quiet room, stable internet, and headphones recommended.

### Scheduling & Punctuality
- Weekly appointments recommended
- Late arrivals over **1/3 of class time** count as a no-show:
  - 10 min late for a 30-min session
  - 15 min late for a 45-min session
  - 20 min late for a 60-min session

### Cancellations & Validity
- Non-refundable once started
- **12+ hours notice:** free rescheduling
- **6–12 hours notice:** 50% fee
- **Less than 6 hours notice:** counts as used
- Pause limit: **30 days**

### Recordings & Privacy
- Lessons recorded for quality control and training
- Accessible for **30 days** before deletion
- Short clips may be used for marketing (no personal info)
- Opt-out fully supported

### Homework Policy
- Available to supplement learning, but **not mandatory**
- Assigned custom-tailored when reinforcement is needed

## Part 6 — About the Method & Curriculum Alignment

> *Add these talking points to the **About Us** or **Our Philosophy** section to highlight the Coach's unique edge and long-term vision.*

### 1. Our Vision: Accompanying Growth
At Exprimio, English is not just a subject to be memorized; it is a tool for life. We focus on building a child's confidence, critical thinking, and willingness to express themselves. Our coaching goes beyond grammar to help children feel relaxed, engaged, and supported.

### 2. A Truly International Perspective
Based in a vibrant, international environment, our coaching brings real-world cultural context into the digital classroom. We serve families looking for more than just language acquisition — we provide an expansive, global lens that helps children connect with the world.

### 3. Long-Term Milestones: KET / PET and IELTS Preparation
Our expression-first methodology naturally lays the groundwork for high-level English exams.
- We integrate targeted development of speaking and writing skills aligned with **KET, PET, and IELTS** standards.
- As students progress, exam-specific elements are introduced seamlessly into 1-on-1 and group coaching programs — preparing children for future academic and international paths without unnecessary stress.

### 4. Communication & Progress Reporting (Operational Update)
To ensure high-quality tracking and respect everyone's time, parents receive **structured, periodic progress feedback** from the Coach at fixed intervals during the package duration. This ensures clear, meaningful communication about the student's growth.
